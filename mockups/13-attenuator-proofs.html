The return path is now drawn the way an engineer would draw it: out of the regulator, two square corners, back up into the source of the signals. Anyone who has ever seen a control-systems block diagram reads this as feedback in half a second — the orthogonal line is the genre convention. The contrast does the storytelling: the world arrives as five thin scattered lines; the response goes back as one thick deliberate one.

The most radical revision: the loop isn't a line added to the diagram — the loop is the circle itself. The thin green lower rim is the world; chords of variety rise off it into the gold regulator; the thick terracotta upper rim carries the response back around. Everything happens inside one closed system, which is the most cybernetic statement of the three. Bonus: it's a circle, so it sits in a favicon and a social avatar perfectly.
